SuperXSecret Privacy Policy

100% Offline • Zero-Knowledge • Zero Network Permissions

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

At SuperXSecret, your privacy is absolute. We built SuperXSecret on a fundamental principle: the only completely secure data is data that never leaves your physical device.

TL;DR - The Zero-Knowledge Guarantee

SuperXSecret does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, secrets, passwords, files, or telemetry. The application does not even request the Android INTERNET permission.

1. Zero Data Collection

SuperXSecret collects NO personal information or device telemetry:

  • No Account Required: You never register, log in, or provide an email address.
  • No Analytics: No tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.).
  • No Crash Reports: No telemetry is transmitted off your phone.
  • No Advertising: No advertising networks or tracking identifiers.

2. Zero Network Connectivity

SuperXSecret explicitly removes android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE from its manifest. The operating system prevents the application from creating network sockets or contacting external servers under any circumstances.

3. Local Cryptography & Data Storage

All records, notes, passwords, and media files are encrypted locally on your device using military-grade authenticated encryption (AES-256-GCM, XChaCha20-Poly1305, or Dual-Cipher) with Argon2id Key Derivation. Your Master Password never leaves memory and is zeroized upon lock.

4. No Password Recovery (Zero-Knowledge Architecture)

Because we do not operate any servers and hold no master decryption keys, we cannot reset or recover your password if forgotten. Users are solely responsible for memorizing their password and keeping regular offline backups (.sxs) on secure physical media.

5. Android Cloud Backup Exclusion

To prevent accidental leaks to third-party cloud providers (such as Google Drive automatic app backups), SuperXSecret explicitly sets android:allowBackup="false" and android:fullBackupContent="false".
